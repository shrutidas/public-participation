### New proposal recommendation (GenX)

Name: **Put the public inside the approval review**
Method: **Stakeholder participation in initial review**
Anchor suggestion: `'insufficient information and potential unreasonable risk'` (the 2009 consent order, the one moment the file identifies where the government simultaneously recognized risk and authorized manufacture). This anchor is shared with "Warn the public when discharge is approved"; the two proposals split the same moment, notice going out against the public coming in.

Description draft: "From the 6/8 session: an FDA-like initial review that involves the public, so approval under uncertainty carries public assurances about how monitoring will happen and who verifies it. The 2009 order set a 99 percent capture requirement and no one checked for eight years; a review with public seats builds the constituency that checks."

Chain links (2 to 3):
1. **Public seats in the review produce monitoring commitments under uncertainty.** Comparable: Finnish and Swedish municipal nuclear waste veto (host communities held formal power at an approval-adjacent decision, with publicly funded independent review; entry below).
2. **A public constituency for the monitoring plan gets it verified.** Counter-evidence already in the file: the EPA OIG finding that the 2009 order contained no scheduled verification and the first inspection came June 28, 2017.
3. **Verified compliance shortens the exposure window and lowers body burden.** Evidence: Kotlarz et al. serum declines of 34 to 65 percent within six months of discharge controls (already cited in the file).

## 3. Evidence detail

Places where the MD carries more specific figures for the same source than the spine shows.

1. **covid-schools.js, "Regional citizen assemblies," Irish comparable.** The MD carries the Suiter, Farrell, Harris, and Murphy analysis: 3,981 speech acts coded; "Members of the Citizens' Assembly demonstrate a deeper cognitively complex grasp of the subject matter" than parliamentarians; experts showed higher cognitive complexity addressing citizens than the parliamentary committee; and the government adopted the assembly's suggested constitutional wording over the parliamentary committee's. None of this is in the spine entry. Add to the outcome paragraph and add the source: `source('Suiter et al. 2022', 'https://journals.sagepub.com/doi/full/10.1177/14789299211020909')`. The expert-complexity finding also strengthens "Bring the right experts together with the public," whose first link currently rests on a weak Texas analogy; it is the one measured experts-with-public result in the MD.

2. **covid-schools.js, "Regional citizen assemblies," French CCC comparable and the binding-remit counter-evidence.** The MD adds: the government "has been selective in prioritising recommendations based on incentives rather than regulation," was more responsive to "recommendations that were more detailed," and roughly 29 percent were dropped. The spine carries only 20 percent, 51 percent, and 3.3 out of 10. Add the selectivity finding; it sharpens what "eroded" means.

3. **covid-vaccines.js, "Public deliberation before any mandate," Korea comparable.** The MD carries: stratified random sampling from a pool of 20,000 across 160 strata; deliberation September 13 to October 15, 2017; President Moon announced resumption October 22, 2017. The research file adds that 53.2 percent simultaneously advocated reducing nuclear power long term. The spine says only "471 citizens deliberated for a month." Add the sampling design; it is the direct answer to the feasibility question the schools chain raises about representative selection at speed.

4. **genx-pfas.js, "Let residents report early warning signs," Tonawanda comparable.** The MD and research file carry: 68 percent benzene reduction at the residential monitor, and the excess-cancer-risk estimate at the industrial monitor falling from 75-in-one-million to 11-in-one-million. The spine has only the 86 percent industrial figure. Also flag: the spine's phrase "the state study confirmed levels 75 times the guideline" looks like a garble of the 75-in-one-million risk figure; verify against the DEC fact sheet before it ships.

5. **genx-pfas.js, "Let residents report early warning signs," Flint comparable.** The MD carries the 84 percent kit return rate (252 of 300 kits). The spine says "269 homes sampled," which matches neither number; reconcile, and add the return rate, since the participation rate is itself the participatory-science point.

6. **genx-pfas.js, "Let the public choose experts," C8 comparable and link evidence.** The MD carries: the panel was "chosen jointly by the attorneys representing the residents and lawyers representing DuPont," and the C8 Health Project collected blood samples from about 69,000 people. The spine has the bilateral appointment in the authority line but not the 69,000-person cohort; add it, since enrollment scale is the assurance-under-uncertainty machinery the workshop asked about.

7. **genx-pfas.js, "Warn the public when discharge is approved," TRI comparable.** The MD carries EPA's Teitelbaum analysis: on-site air releases of TRI-reportable chemicals down 52 percent, or 838 million pounds, 2003 to 2013, self-reported by facilities. The spine cites a different EPA page with a 21 percent figure since 2014. Add the Teitelbaum figure and source URL (https://www.epa.gov/sites/default/files/2015-09/documents/teitelbaum.pdf); the two are compatible windows and the MD's is the larger measured decline.

8. **covid-vaccines.js, "Let communities design how vaccines reach them," Uganda comparable.** The research file carries the precision the spine omits: 90 percent CI of 8 to 64 percent on the mortality reduction, roughly 550 averted deaths, infant weight-for-age up 0.14 z. The wide interval is also the honest bridge to the weaker replication the spine already mentions.

9. **covid-vaccines.js, "Fund evaluation alongside the programs," Progresa comparable.** The MD carries Behrman's conclusion that estimated benefit-cost ratios of IFPRI's evaluation considerably exceed one, which is the one figure about the evaluation itself rather than the program; the research file adds secondary enrollment up 11 percent for girls and 7.5 percent for boys. The Behrman line belongs in the entry, since the proposal is about buying evaluation, not cash transfers. Source: `source('Behrman 2010', 'https://ideas.repec.org/a/eee/wdevel/v38y2010i10p1473-1485.html')`.

**PMC essay check.** The normative essay "Deliberation, Citizen Science and Covid-19" (PMC7361354) appears nowhere in the three spine files. The only pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov citations are PMC8938683 (fall 2020 modality shares, covid-schools.js) and PMC9663766 (Calgary caries study, proposed above), both empirical. No action needed; keep it out.
