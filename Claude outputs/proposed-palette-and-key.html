Key

Every box on the map opens in the side panel when clicked. Read left to right: what the record measured, what happened, what is proposed, and what the proposal is argued to do.

The record

Timeline event
One dated event, in order. The dot names its category.
Policy Events Knowledge Public opinion Public participation (actual)
★
Existing mechanism
An oversight or participation channel that already existed and should have caught this event.
Measured outcome
An endpoint the record measured, placed beside the event it follows from.

The proposals

Proposed participation
A proposal from the design work, placed beside the part of the record it speaks to. Dashed because it did not happen.
Proposed implication
The one outcome a proposal argues for, in a sentence. Green because it names an outcome; selecting it lights the measured outcome it matches.

The support

Claim
A general statement in support of the implication. Not a step in a chain.
Evidence
What one source found, in a plain sentence, with the source beneath.
Counter
Evidence against the claim.
Case study
A real case where something like this ran.
