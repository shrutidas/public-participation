Case Study 1  ·  COVID-19 School Closures and Reopenings
Measured Outcome
Confidence in public schools

Gallup confidence fell from 41% in 2020 to 28% in 2022, with the drop concentrated among Republicans.

Public school enrollment

Enrollment fell by 1.4 million students between fall 2019 and fall 2020 and has not recovered.

Chronic absenteeism

Roughly 22% of students missed a tenth or more of school days through 2024-25.

Test scores

Scores fell sharply after the pandemic; recent studies show the decline began years earlier.

Infections and deaths

To be added: the analysis of how closures affected infection and death rates.
